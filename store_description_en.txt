【Automate your bookmark organization.】

Have links you saved for "read later" gotten lost in the clutter? Date Bookmark Manager is a simple yet powerful Chrome extension that automatically organizes your bookmarks by the date they were saved, boosting your productivity and information management.

■ Recommended for those who:
・Have a bookmark bar overflowing with unorganized links
・Want to review their research or saved pages grouped by date
・Find manual folder organization too tedious to maintain

■ Key Features
・Daily Saving: Simply right-click and select "Bookmark to Date Folder". The link will be instantly filed into today's folder.
・Monthly Auto-Archiving: When the month changes, the previous month's daily folders are automatically bundled into a "YYYY-MM" monthly folder. This keeps your bookmark bar perfectly clean and tidy.
・Customizable Settings: Access the extension's "Options" page to tailor it exactly to your workflow.
  - Custom Date Formats: Choose from 6 different date styles (e.g., yyyy-MM-dd, yyyyMMdd, MMDD) to suit your preference.
  - Multi-language Support: Easily change the context menu language (Japanese / English / 简体中文 / 한국어) directly from the settings.
  - Group Current Month Instantly (New): Choose to always create and organize daily folders directly inside the current month's folder, rather than waiting for the month to change.

■ How to Use
1. Right-click on any page or link you want to save.
2. Select "Bookmark to Date Folder".

That's it! Your bookmarks will be beautifully organized along a clear timeline! For advanced customization, simply right-click the extension icon in your browser and select "Options".
